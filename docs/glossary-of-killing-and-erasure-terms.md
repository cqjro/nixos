# Comprehensive Glossary of Killing, Erasure, and Destruction Terms

This document compiles every term and definition discussed throughout our conversation, organized by category and etymological origin.

---

## 1. General Verbs, Methods, and Contexts of Killing

### By Physical Method
* **Asphyxiate / Smother:** To kill someone by depriving them of oxygen, often by covering the mouth and nose.
* **Butcher / Slaughter:** To kill in a brutally violent, bloody, or indiscriminate manner.
* **Decapitate / Behead:** To kill by completely severing the head from the body.
* **Defenestrate:** To throw someone out of a window, historically associated with political assassinations.
* **Electrocute:** To kill through a fatal electric shock.
* **Exsanguinate:** To kill by draining a person of blood.
* **Guillotine:** To behead someone using a heavy falling blade.
* **Immersion / Drowning:** To kill by submerging someone in liquid until they suffocate.
* **Strangle / Garrote:** To kill by constricting the neck, with *garrote* specifically referring to the use of a wire, cord, or metal collar.

### By Scale, Motive, or Context
* **Assassinate:** To murder a prominent, political, or public figure, usually by surprise or for ideological reasons.
* **Dispatch:** To kill quickly and efficiently.
* **Euthanize:** To painlessly end a life to relieve suffering from an incurable condition (also called mercy killing).
* **Execute:** To put someone to death as a legal penalty or formal sentence.
* **Lynch:** To publicly kill someone without due process, typically by a mob.
* **Massacre:** To indiscriminately and brutally kill a large number of people at one time.

### Slang and Euphemisms
* **Organized Crime Slang (*Whack, Rub out, Bump off, Off, Ice, Hit*):** Informal terms referring to targeted contract killings.
* **Military and Intelligence Euphemisms (*Neutralize, Liquidate, Eliminate, Terminate, Wet work*):** Terms used to describe covert or tactical killings.

## 2. Established Dictionary & Academic "-cide" Terms

### Family and Domestic Relationships
* **Avunculicide:** Killing an uncle.
* **Familiaricide:** Killing an entire family, often by one of its own members (also called family annihilation).
* **Filicide:** Killing one's own child.
* **Fratricide:** Killing one's brother, or accidentally killing fellow soldiers in combat.
* **Infanticide:** Killing an infant under one year old.
* **Mariticide:** Killing one's husband.
* **Matricide:** Killing one's own mother.
* **Neonaticide:** Killing a newborn within the first 24 hours of life.
* **Nepoticide:** Killing a nephew.
* **Parricide:** Killing a parent or another close relative.
* **Patricide:** Killing one's own father.
* **Prolicide:** Killing one's own offspring.
* **Sororicide:** Killing one's own sister.
* **Uxoricide:** Killing one's wife.

### Social, Political, and Religious Figures
* **Ambassadicide:** Killing an ambassador.
* **Amicicide:** Killing a friend.
* **Deicide:** Killing a god or divine being (from Latin *deus*).
* **Episcopicide:** Killing a bishop.
* **Hospiticide:** Killing a guest or a host.
* **Magnicide:** Killing a prominent, high-ranking, or famous public figure.
* **Pontificide:** Killing a pope or high priest.
* **Presbytericide:** Killing a priest or elder.
* **Regicide:** Killing a monarch, king, or queen.
* **Tyrannicide:** Killing a tyrant or oppressive ruler.
* **Vaticide:** Killing a prophet or seer.

### Age and Demographics
* **Androcide:** The systematic killing of men or boys.
* **Femicide / Gynecide:** Killing women or girls specifically because of their gender.
* **Gendercide:** The systematic killing of members of a specific gender.
* **Geronticide:** Killing old people.
* **Paedocide:** Killing children.
* **Senicide:** Killing the elderly, often through abandonment or ritual practices.

### Erasure, Memory, and Abstract Concepts
* **Chronocide:** The destruction or "killing" of time; historically used to describe wasting time and used in speculative fiction for destroying timelines.
* **Culturicide / Ethnocide:** The systematic destruction of a people's culture, traditions, and way of life rather than their physical bodies.
* **Epistemicide:** A sociological term for the systematic destruction or erasure of an entire system of knowledge or way of understanding the world (from Greek *episteme*, "knowledge").
* **Famicide:** An archaic legal and literary term for destroying someone's reputation, good name, or public standing through slander.
* **Liberticide:** The destruction of liberty, freedom, or constitutional rights.
* **Linguicide:** The intentional killing or forced extinction of a language (from Latin *lingua*, "tongue/language").
* **Memoricide:** The deliberate destruction of collective memory, historical records, or cultural heritage to erase a group's past from existence.
* **Menticide:** The systematic undermining and destruction of a person's mind, beliefs, and free will through brainwashing or psychological torture.
* **Pseudocide:** The act of faking one's own death to disappear from society and start a new life, effectively "killing" one's former identity.
* **Scholasticide:** The systematic destruction of education, schools, and scholarly infrastructure.
* **Scienticide:** The suppression, destruction, or killing of science and scientific inquiry.
* **Verbicide:** The deliberate distortion, perversion, or "killing" of a word's true meaning.

### Mass, World, and Cosmic Destruction
* **Biocide:** The destruction of all living organisms or life itself.
* **Cosmocide:** The destruction of the universe or cosmos (from Greek *kosmos*, "order/universe").
* **Democide:** The murder of any person or people by their government.
* **Ecocide:** The extensive destruction of the natural environment or ecosystems.
* **Genocide:** The deliberate and systematic destruction of a national, ethnic, racial, or religious group.
* **Geocide:** The destruction of the Earth or a massive geographical region.
* **Homicide:** The killing of one human being by another, whether lawful or unlawful.
* **Mundicide:** The destruction of a world or planet.
* **Omnicide:** The total destruction of all human life or all life, causing extinction.
* **Politicide:** The deliberate destruction of a group based on their political affiliation or ideology.
* **Sociocide:** The destruction of a society or social order.
* **Specicide:** The complete destruction or extinction of an entire species.
* **Urbicide:** The deliberate destruction or "killing" of a city, often used in urban studies and warfare history.

### Self and Specific Methods
* **Autocide:** Killing oneself; most commonly defined in modern forensics as suicide via a motor vehicle, or used broadly for self-consuming systems.
* **Lapidicide:** Killing someone by stoning.
* **Parasuicide:** An act that mimics suicide—such as severe self-harm or a non-fatal gesture—without the actual intent to end one's life.
* **Suicide:** The act of intentionally ending one's own life.
* **Veneficide:** Killing someone by poisoning (also stems from *veneficus*, "sorcerer," implying the killing of a witch or warlock).

## 3. Speculative, Mythological, and Coined "-cide" Terms

*Note: These terms are formed from valid Latin and Greek roots for creative writing, worldbuilding, and philosophical speculation, though most are not standard dictionary entries.*

### Divine, Celestial, and Mythological Entities
* **Angelicide:** The killing of an angel or celestial messenger.
* **Chimericide:** The slaying of a chimera or hybrid beast.
* **Daemonicide / Demonicide:** The slaying of a demon or evil spirit.
* **Diabolicide:** The killing of a devil or archfiend.
* **Draconicide:** The slaying of a dragon.
* **Fayicide:** The killing of a fairy or fae creature.
* **Giganticide:** The killing of a giant or titan.
* **Gorgonicide:** The slaying of a gorgon.
* **Hydricide:** The killing of a hydra or multi-headed serpent.
* **Idolicide:** The destruction of a false god, idol, or divine vessel.
* **Lycanthropicide:** The killing of a werewolf or shapeshifter.
* **Magicide:** The killing of a mage, wizard, or magic user.
* **Monstrocide:** The slaying of a monster or beast.
* **Necrocide:** The killing or permanent destruction of the undead.
* **Sacrilegicide:** The destruction or violation of sacred objects or places.
* **Sancticide:** The destruction of a saint or something sacred.
* **Seraphicide:** The killing of a seraph or high-ranking angel.
* **Sirenide / Sirenicide:** The killing of a siren or mermaid.
* **Spectricide:** The destruction or banishing of a ghost or spectre.
* **Theocide:** The killing of a god (from Greek *theos*, "god").
* **Titanicide:** The slaying of a titan or primordial giant.
* **Vampiricide:** The killing of a vampire.

### Metaphysics, Reality, and Cosmic Scale
* **Aethericide:** The destruction of the fundamental magical or celestial medium that binds the universe together (from Greek *aither*, "upper air/pure essence").
* **Aeviternicide:** The destruction of eternity or perpetual existence, making the immortal subject to decay (from Latin *aeviternitas*, "eternity/age").
* **Anachronocide:** Coined from *anachronism*, meaning the killing of something out of its proper time or rendering it obsolete.
* **Astroide / Stellicide:** The destruction of a star or celestial body.
* **Essenticide:** The destruction of a thing's core nature or fundamental truth, leaving behind a hollow shell (from Latin *essentia*, "being/essence").
* **Faticide:** The killing of destiny or fate, severing a being or world from its predetermined path (from Latin *fatum*, "fate").
* **Onticide:** The destruction of being or existence itself, erasing something so thoroughly it ceases to have ever been real (from Greek *ontos*, "being").
* **Planeticide:** The destruction of an entire planet.
* **Solacide:** The extinguishing or destruction of a sun.
* **Technocide:** The destruction of an older technology or craft by making it obsolete.
* **Telosicide:** The destruction of ultimate purpose, destiny, or end goals, leaving reality directionless (from Greek *telos*, "end/purpose").
* **Temporicide:** The destruction of an entire era, age, or timeline.

### Mind, Meaning, and Conceptual Destruction
* **Animicide:** The destruction of a soul or vital spirit.
* **Iconocide:** The destruction of sacred images, symbols, or the foundational archetypes they represent (from Greek *eikon*, "image").
* **Ideacide:** The eradication of an idea, belief, or concept.
* **Logocide:** The destruction of reason, logic, or the divine word that orders the universe (from Greek *logos*, "word/reason").
* **Luminicide:** The extinguishing of light, often used metaphorically for hope or knowledge.
* **Memeticide:** The destruction of a self-replicating idea, memory, or cultural concept.
* **Mnemocide:** The deliberate erasure or murder of memory, either from an individual mind or from collective history (from Greek *mneme*, "memory").
* **Mythicide:** The destruction of foundational myths, legends, or sacred truths that give a culture its identity (from Greek *mythos*, "story/myth").
* **Noeticide:** The destruction of intellect, thought, or collective consciousness (from Greek *noetikos*, "intellectual").
* **Oneiricide:** The killing of dreams, or the destruction of a dream-realm and the ability to imagine (from Greek *oneiros*, "dream").
* **Onomasticide:** The erasure or "killing" of a name, stripping a being of its true identity.
* **Psychicide:** The destruction of the soul, psyche, or seat of consciousness (from Greek *psykhe*, "soul/mind").
* **Semanticide:** The destruction of meaning itself, stripping words, symbols, and actions of any significance (from Greek *semantikos*, "significant").
* **Spiriticide:** The annihilation of a spirit or ghost.
* **Veriticide:** The murder of objective truth, rendering reality entirely subjective or deceptive (from Latin *veritas*, "truth").

### Erasure, Self-Destruction, and Void
* **Amnesicide:** The total murder or erasure of all memory, leaving a person or world completely forgotten (from Greek *amnesia*, "forgetfulness").
* **Egocide:** A psychological and philosophical term for the death or total dismantling of the ego and sense of individual self.
* **Evanescide:** The act of causing something to vanish or fade away like vapor until nothing remains (from Latin *evanescere*, "to vanish").
* **Historicide:** The deliberate erasure of history, removing an event, person, or civilization from the timeline of record.
* **Identicide:** The systematic erasure of a person's or group's identity, leaving them hollowed out and unrecognizable.
* **Ipsicide:** The destruction of one's own unique identity or "selfhood" (from Latin *ipse*, "self/very one").
* **Letheicide:** The destruction of memory through absolute oblivion, named after Lethe, the mythological Greek river of forgetfulness.
* **Nihilicide:** The reduction of something into absolute nothingness, erasing both matter and memory (from Latin *nihil*, "nothing").
* **Obscuricide:** The deliberate casting of something into total darkness and unknowability, ensuring it is never found or remembered (from Latin *obscurus*, "dark/hidden").
* **Silenticide:** The destruction of voice and sound, consigning an entity to eternal, unspoken obscurity (from Latin *silentium*, "silence").
* **Suigenericide:** Literally "the killing of one's own kind" or "destroying something entirely unique" (from Latin *sui generis*, "of its own kind").
* **Umbracide:** The destruction of a shadow or lingering reflection, severing a being's final, faintest tie to the physical world (from Latin *umbra*, "shadow").
* **Vacuicide:** The destruction of substance, leaving behind an empty void where a person, soul, or concept used to be (from Latin *vacuus*, "empty").
* **Vestigicide:** The destruction of every trace, footprint, or remaining sign that something ever existed (from Latin *vestigium*, "footprint/trace").

## 4. Related Non-"-cide" Terms

* **Atrophy:** A medical and figurative term for wasting away or declining in effectiveness due to underuse or neglect.
* **Chronoclasm:** A catastrophic shattering of timelines and causality (uses *-clasm*, "breaking").
* **Desuetude:** A formal noun meaning the state of discontinuance or disuse, describing a law, custom, or word that has quietly "died" because people stopped using it.
* **Extinction:** The complete dying out of a species, tradition, language, or concept so that no examples remain.
* **Moribund:** An adjective from the Latin *mori* ("to die") meaning at the point of death or lacking vitality, often describing an outdated industry or tradition.
* **Obsolescence:** The process of becoming obsolete and falling out of use (from Latin *obsolescere*, "to grow old" or "wear out").
* **Senescence:** The biological or conceptual process of growing old and deteriorating toward the end of a lifespan.
